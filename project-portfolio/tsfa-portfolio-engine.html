TSFA Dual Market V1.3

LIVE APPLICATION - V1.3

A dual-market portfolio management and financial engineering platform designed to support investment decisions through transaction-based portfolio tracking, performance measurement, risk analysis, stress testing, and portfolio allocation.

Research and decision-support platform for Indonesian and United States equity markets.

Project Context

Managing an equity portfolio requires more than monitoring market prices. Investors need a consistent record of transactions, reliable cost-basis calculations, performance measurement, risk attribution, and disciplined allocation decisions.

TSFA Dual Market V1.3 is now deployed as an integrated portfolio analysis application, bringing transaction-based portfolio tracking, performance measurement, risk analysis, stress testing, and allocation tools into one workflow.

Core Capabilities

  • Transaction-based portfolio tracking: portfolio holdings and cash are derived from recorded transactions, with opening balances available when historical records are incomplete.
  • Portfolio accounting: moving weighted-average cost methodology and share quantities recorded in units.
  • Performance analytics: portfolio return, drawdown, volatility, and related performance indicators.
  • Risk analytics: portfolio risk contribution, correlation analysis, and risk assessment.
  • Portfolio construction: target allocation and rebalancing analysis.
  • Sector analysis: sector exposure and sector-level holdings assessment.
  • Stress testing: market and sector scenario analysis to evaluate potential portfolio impact.
  • Excel reporting: downloadable analysis workbook containing 13 structured worksheets.

System Architecture

Market data and transaction records → data validation → portfolio accounting engine → performance and risk analytics → allocation and stress testing → interactive dashboard and Excel reporting.

Excel Reporting Structure

The V1.3 reporting workflow is designed to export portfolio analysis into a structured workbook with 13 worksheets:

  • HOLDINGS
  • ANALYSIS_RESULTS
  • PORTFOLIO_SUMMARY
  • VALIDATION_NOTES
  • RISK_ANALYSIS
  • RISK_CONTRIBUTION
  • CORRELATION_MATRIX
  • TARGET_ALLOCATION
  • REBALANCING_ANALYSIS
  • SECTOR_EXPOSURE
  • SECTOR_HOLDINGS
  • MARKET_STRESS
  • SECTOR_STRESS

Data Privacy & Deployment Scope

Portfolio uploads are intended to be processed separately from shared market data and the public source-code repository. Personal holdings and transaction records are not included in this public project page or repository.

The V1.3 application is publicly deployed. Portfolio uploads are processed in the Streamlit session and are not included in the public source-code repository. This is a decision-support tool, not audited investment performance or a guarantee of investment returns.
